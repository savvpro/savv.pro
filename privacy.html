▸ 00 / Privacy last revised · 2026-05-19

what we collect.
why. how to remove it.

SavvPro operates remote-first and resource-agnostic. We collect the minimum data needed to have a useful conversation with you. No third-party analytics, no advertising trackers, no cross-site profiling.

▸ 01 / What we collect explicit · enumerable
name if you give it to the agent
email if you give it to the agent
phone_or_linkedin optional · 'skip' accepted
conversation_transcript what you type to the agent
browser_local_storage 'savv-intake' (your name/email only)
server_logs IP + user-agent · 30-day rolling

We do not run third-party analytics, ad pixels, session recorders, or fingerprinting. We do not sell or share your data with anyone outside SavvPro.

▸ 02 / Why stated purpose · no drift

The intake exists so the conversation has continuity — so we can reply, so a contributor pathway can move forward, and so we can recognize you on a return visit. The legal basis under GDPR is your consent (you typed it into the terminal) and our legitimate interest in operating an inbound channel.

We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making about employment, or any purpose other than the conversation you initiated.

▸ 03 / Retention finite · auditable
conversation_transcripts 24 months · then purged
contact_records until you ask for removal
server_logs 30 days · rolling
browser_local_storage you control · clear site data
▸ 04 / Who sees it first-party only

The agent on this site is SavvPro's own infrastructure, operated by SavvPro. Your conversation is not routed through a third-party AI vendor for storage or training. Hosting and email delivery use standard infrastructure providers (compute, DNS, email) acting only as processors under contract.

▸ 05 / Your rights GDPR · CCPA · plain
01 · access we send you what we have
02 · correction we fix what is wrong
03 · deletion we remove it
04 · portability we export it as JSON
05 · objection we stop processing

To exercise any of the above, email privacy@savv.pro from the address you used in intake. We respond within 30 days.

▸ 06 / Cookies none of the usual ones

This site sets no cookies. It uses browser localStorage for one purpose only — remembering your name and email so you do not redo the intake on a return visit. Clearing site data in your browser removes it instantly. No third party reads this storage.

If something on this page is unclear or feels off, tell us. Privacy that needs a footnote is not privacy.
