This page self-selects. The people who read it and still want to apply are exactly the people we want. We are not measuring credentials. We are measuring the signal you give off and the discipline you bring.

▸ 01 / Environment how savvpro is built
01

Remote-first

Distributed by default. Performance is measured in contribution and quality, not hours or office presence.

02

Resource-agnostic

The work is composed from Capabilities, not from who is on which team. Any contributor — human or AI agent — with the required skills executes.

03

Agent-first

AI tools are core to the work, not an experiment. You use them daily, you build with them, you operate alongside them.

04

No hierarchy

Three roles only — Individual Contributor, Directly Responsible Individual, Player-Coach. Accountability without administrative layers.

▸ 02 / Traits tier 1 · non-negotiable
01

Truth Signal Quality

You report what is actually happening, not what is comfortable. Your delivery data is accurate. Your status is honest. The World Model depends on this.

▸ Not a fit if

Your default move is to round the estimate or omit the uncomfortable signal.

02

Integrity Under Pressure

When the work is hard, the commitments hold. When something goes wrong, you flag it early. The discipline does not wobble when no one is watching.

▸ Not a fit if

Pressure changes what you say is true.

03

Learning Velocity

You convert real-world signal into improved capability faster than the room average. You read the doctrine and update your operating model the same week.

▸ Not a fit if

You need a six-month ramp to feel competent.
